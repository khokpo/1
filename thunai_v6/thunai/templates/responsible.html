Responsible use

Support, not labelling.

  • THUNAI is a decision-support system. It does not diagnose students, and it does not replace teachers: technology identifies patterns, educators make decisions.
  • It never labels a student as failed, weak or poor. A signal reads “Attention Recommended” or “Priority Support” and is a prompt for a conversation.
  • It does not determine academic failure and never triggers punishment or exclusion.
  • All signals require human review. Current signals are prototype statistical early-support rules, not a validated predictive model.
  • The Academic Momentum Index weights are a prototype configuration: institutional validation is required. Students do not see a score; they see their progress and the support available.
  • Improvement is reported as “indicators improved after the support”, not as proof of causation.
  • Students can request human support at any time. Role-based access applies: a student sees their own information, faculty see their students, a mentor sees assigned mentees, the HOD sees authorised department data, and the office does not see mentoring conversations.
