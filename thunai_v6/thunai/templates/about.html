How THUNAI works

ERP tells us what happened. THUNAI helps decide what support should happen next.

The support loop

  1. ObserveAttendance, internal marks, model exams and assignments arrive from your ERP or LMS.
  2. IdentifyPatterns across several indicators raise an Academic Support Signal. One low mark alone does not.
  3. UnderstandThe mentor talks with the student and records the need. The system never assumes the cause.
  4. InterveneTutorials, doubt-clearing, peer learning, mentor meetings or study planning, matched to the need.
  5. Follow upA follow-up date is set and completed, so support does not stop after one conversation.
  6. MeasureBefore and after indicators show whether the support is working, then the cycle continues.

Support status, in plain words

Stable

No action needed.

Monitor

Worth keeping an eye on.

Attention Recommended

A mentor conversation is advised.

Priority Support

Prompt, personal support is advised.

Who sees what

RoleWhat they do in THUNAI
StudentSees own attendance, marks, assignments and progress, and asks for support. No risk score is shown.
Subject facultyEnters attendance and assessments, manages assignments, views their students.
MentorSees assigned mentees, understands the need, records support, follows up.
HODDepartment overview: signals, active support, improvement, follow-ups due.
OfficeManages records, assigns mentors and configures structures. Does not see mentoring conversations.

Alongside your ERP and LMS

THUNAI does not replace your ERP or LMS. They store and deliver. THUNAI adds the missing layer between academic data and student support: signal, mentor, intervention, follow-up and outcome.

Technology identifies patterns; educators make decisions. See Responsible use.
